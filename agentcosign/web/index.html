AgentCoSign

When an AI assistant wants to refund a customer or pay someone, it has to ask first. Clear rules decide what is safe. You approve the rest with your fingerprint or screen lock. Every step is written to a record that shows if anyone changed it.

Try it in two minutes

Open the app

  1. Set up with your fingerprint, face or PIN. You get your own private test space; your fingerprint never leaves your device.
  2. Press Try a scenario. Each scenario creates a real PayPal sandbox order and sends a customer's message about it. The AI checks read the message, then the rules decide. Or write your own customer message.
  3. Switch on Behind the scenes to watch every step on the server as it happens: each PayPal call, each AI check, the decision and the signed record.

Works on a phone or a laptop. On the free plan the first visit can take about a minute to wake the demo, and test data is cleared when it sleeps.

Also open to look at

  • The limits the demo enforces: the amount refunded without asking, the amount that needs a person, and the daily cap.
  • The latest record entry: publish it, and anyone can later check that no earlier entry was changed.
  • The API reference: read every call and try it from your browser.
  • Service health: whether the demo is running and how it is serving requests.
